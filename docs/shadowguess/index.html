ShadowGuessKids

A guessing game built for toddlers and preschoolers. They look at a silhouette and guess the object — animals, sea life, fruits and vegetables, vehicles, instruments, and more. The whole game runs offline, so no account is needed and nothing leaves the device.

14 categories

Animals, sea life, fruits & vegetables, vehicles, instruments, nature, and more — each with multiple semantic objects and varied silhouettes.

Two play modes

Tap-to-answer for quiet play, or the "Surprise Face" video voice mode where the device reacts to the child's voice and expression.

Bilingual labels

Both Chinese and English names are available, so play doubles as early language exposure.

No ads, no in-app purchases

No ads, no pop-ups, no upsells. Nothing is uploaded. The background music is synthesized on the device.

影子猜猜乐

专为幼儿与学龄前儿童设计、无任何联网功能的影子识物小游戏。孩子观察剪影轮廓，猜出动物、果蔬、交通工具等日常事物，在轻松游戏中认识世界、锻炼观察力与语言表达。

14 大主题

动物、海洋、果蔬、交通工具、乐器、自然等，每类含多个语义对象与多样造型变体。

两种玩法

点击答题，适合安静环境；「惊喜脸」视频语音模式，会回应孩子的表情与声音。

中英文双语

提供中文与英文两种称谓，边玩边做认知启蒙。

无广告、无内购

不含广告、弹窗或内购，不上传任何数据；背景音乐由设备实时合成。
