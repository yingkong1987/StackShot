macOS · Clipboard manager

StackPaste

A clipboard manager built for creators, developers, and power users. It quietly records everything you copy — text, links, images, files, and colors — into a searchable history, so you can find it again in seconds.

Searchable history

Text, links, images, files, and colors are captured in the background and kept in a searchable history on your Mac.

Stacks & sequential paste

Group related clips into a Stack, copy several items at once, then paste them one after another in order — without switching between apps.

Keyboard first

Quick filters, a keyboard-first workflow, and custom shortcuts help you find and paste what you need fast.

Private by design

Your clipboard data stays on your Mac and syncs only through your own personal iCloud account.

macOS · 剪贴板管理

StackPaste

为创作者、开发者和高阶用户打造的剪贴板管理工具。它会在后台自动记录你复制的所有内容，并把文本、链接、图片、文件和颜色保存在可搜索的历史记录中，让你几秒内重新找到它们。

可搜索的历史记录

文本、链接、图片、文件和颜色会在后台被记录，并保存在 Mac 上可搜索的历史记录中。

Stacks 与顺序粘贴

把相关内容整理到 Stacks，一次复制多个项目，再按顺序逐个粘贴，不必在应用之间来回切换。

键盘优先

借助快速筛选、键盘优先的操作方式与自定义快捷键，你可以迅速找到并粘贴所需内容。

隐私优先的设计

剪贴板数据会私密保存在你的 Mac 上，只会通过你个人的 iCloud 进行同步。
