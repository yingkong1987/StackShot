StackPaste Privacy Policy

This policy explains how StackPaste handles your clipboard data. The short version: your history is stored locally in an encrypted database, iCloud sync is optional and encrypted, and we do not run a server that holds your clips.

Where your data is stored

Clipboard history is saved by default in a local encrypted database (SQLCipher) on your Mac. It does not leave your device unless you turn on sync.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, clips are encrypted before being synced to your own iCloud account (CloudKit). The keys exist only on your devices — the developer cannot read your content.

On-device OCR

Text recognition runs on your device. Image content is not uploaded for processing.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No account is required to use the core features.
  • No advertising SDKs and no behavioral analytics.
  • No automatic upload of your clipboard content to a developer-operated server.
  • We never sell your clipboard history or usage habits.

Third-party services

  • Apple CloudKit — used only for optional iCloud sync; data lives in your own iCloud account and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy.
  • Apple frameworks — on-device OCR and translation. Per Apple's framework documentation, Apple may process usage and performance metrics that do not include your original text, such as the app bundle identifier and source/target languages.
  • GitHub Pages — this public page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic web request logs under its own policies.

StackPaste 隐私政策

本政策说明 StackPaste 如何处理你的剪贴板数据。简要结论：历史记录默认保存在本机加密数据库中，iCloud 同步为可选项且经过加密，我们不运营存储你剪贴内容的服务器。

数据保存在哪里

剪贴板历史默认保存在本机加密数据库（SQLCipher）中。除非你主动开启同步，数据不会离开你的设备。

可选的 iCloud 同步

若你开启 iCloud 同步，片段会先加密再同步到你自己的 iCloud 账户（CloudKit），密钥仅存在于你的设备，开发者无法读取。

设备端 OCR

文字识别在设备端完成，图像内容不会为了识别而上传。

购买与订阅

内购与订阅完全由 Apple 处理，我们不收集或保存你的支付信息。

我们不会做的事

  • 不会要求注册账户来使用核心功能。
  • 不内置广告 SDK，也不做用户行为统计分析。
  • 不会把你的剪贴板内容自动上传到开发者运营的服务器。
  • 绝不会出售你的剪贴板历史或使用习惯数据。

第三方与外部服务

  • Apple CloudKit：仅用于可选的 iCloud 同步，相关数据保存在你自己的 iCloud 账户中，由 Apple 依据其隐私政策处理。
  • Apple 官方框架：设备端 OCR 与翻译。根据 Apple 的框架说明，Apple 可能处理不包含原文内容的使用与性能指标，例如 App Bundle ID 与源 / 目标语言。
  • GitHub Pages：本公开页面托管于 GitHub Pages。访问本页面时，GitHub 可能依据其自身政策处理基础的网页请求日志。
